Bloodborne Co-op 1.0.0 — mark3317

Start Bloodborne.exe and select your own Bloodborne 1.09 game folder.
For co-op, enter your account and host invite in the launcher.
BBSC, Xbox prompts and PCBButtons are bundled; no separate mod download is needed.
Settings -> Mods & Patches -> Gameplay patches contains one-hit kill and other cheats.
Read README.md (English) or README.ru.md (Russian) for setup and known limitations.
Updates: https://github.com/mark3317/bloodborne_pc/releases
